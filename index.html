How to play

Question set

Write one question per block and leave a blank line between blocks. Use P: for the passage (leave it out to reuse the previous passage), S: for the statement, A: for the answer (T, F or NG), E: for the evidence (optional; wrap words in ==...== to underline and highlight them yellow, or ++...++ for light blue), B: all-in to let teams bet all their money on that question, and O: true-false to show only TRUE and FALSE.
